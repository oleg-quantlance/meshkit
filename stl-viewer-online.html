What the Online STL Viewer Does

Before you slice, share or buy a model you usually want to look at it: is it the right size, is it standing up, is it closed, does it have the detail the thumbnail promised? This viewer opens .stl (binary or ASCII), .3mf (including Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer and Cura project files) and .obj directly in your browser, renders them with WebGL, and runs the same mesh audit as the STL Repair tool: dimensions, triangle count, volume, surface area, open edges, holes, non-manifold edges and whether the normals point outward.

The model is parsed in a Web Worker and drawn on a canvas in this tab. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account — close the tab and it is gone. Files up to 100 MB load in a second or two on a laptop; a few million triangles render smoothly on any machine with a GPU from the last decade.

How to View an STL, 3MF or OBJ in 3 Steps

  1. Drop the file on the upload area, or click one of the three sample buttons to see the viewer working.
  2. Orbit: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag (or shift-drag) to pan. Use the ISO / TOP / FRONT / RIGHT presets to check orientation and FIT to re-frame.
  3. Read the numbers: dimensions in millimetres, volume in cm³ (multiply by your filament's density for grams — or open Volume & Weight), and the mesh-health cell. Toggle Wireframe to inspect triangulation density; save a PNG preview for a listing or a forum post.

Worked Example: Reading the Mesh-Health Cell

WATERTIGHT · 0 holes · 0 non-manifold · normals outward  →  slice it
24 OPEN EDGES · 1 hole  →  an opening (a vase) or a missing face; repair if it should be solid
inside-out  →  signed volume is negative; the viewer tints back-faces orange so the whole shell looks orange
non-manifold > 0  →  an edge shared by 3+ faces; slicers may drop or fill it unpredictably

Volume is computed from signed tetrahedra (the divergence theorem), so it is only meaningful for closed shells; for an open mesh treat it as approximate. Surface area is exact for any triangle soup.

Why a Browser Viewer Instead of a Slicer?

  • Speed: opening a slicer, importing, waiting for it to analyse — versus dropping a file on a tab.
  • Privacy: a client's CAD or a commercial model never leaves your machine; most online viewers upload to a server first.
  • Checking downloads: verify a Printables / Thingiverse / MakerWorld file is what it claims before committing to a print.
  • 3MF projects: see what is actually on the plate — components, transforms and units resolved — without launching the slicer that made it.

Technical Limitations

Colours, textures and materials are not displayed; everything is one flat-shaded material. Multiple objects in a 3MF or OBJ are merged into one view. The viewer requires WebGL; where it is unavailable the dimensions and audit still run and are shown without the 3D preview. Very large files (tens of millions of triangles) depend on your device's memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my file uploaded to view it?

No. The file is read in a Web Worker and rendered with WebGL in this tab. The page makes no network request with your data.

Why is the whole model orange?

Faces whose normals point away from the camera are tinted orange. If every face is orange, the mesh is inside-out (negative signed volume). STL Repair flips it in one click.

The model is lying on its side.

The viewer is Z-up, like printers and slicers. A model that lies down was exported Y-up (Blender, Maya, glTF). For OBJ files, the OBJ to STL converter has a one-click Y-up → Z-up option.

Can I measure a specific feature?

Not yet — the viewer reports overall dimensions, volume and surface area. Point-to-point measurement is on the roadmap.

Does it open 3MF project files from Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer?

Yes. The plate layout, component transforms, Production-extension sub-parts and units are all resolved, and support/modifier objects are skipped, so you see the printable geometry as placed.

Reference // What "Watertight" Means

Every edge is shared by exactly two triangles with consistent winding. Zero boundary edges, zero non-manifold edges, positive signed volume. That is the condition a slicer needs to decide what is inside the part.
