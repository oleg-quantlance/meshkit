  2. Open it; if a yellow bar says Protected View, click Enable Editing.
  3. Excel 2016 or newer, Excel for Microsoft 365, or Excel for Mac 2016+. Older versions open the file but some formulas show #NAME? (see troubleshooting).

Five steps to a tracked shelf

  1. Settings tab. Check the low-stock threshold in grams (default 150 g), the default net spool weight (1,000 g), the fallback tare weight, and the dry-box humidity warning percentage. Customise the Materials and Locations lists so the drop-downs match your shelf.
  2. Spools tab: add a spool. One row per spool (up to 60 rows). Pick the brand and spool type from the drop-down; the empty-spool (tare) weight fills in automatically from the built-in Tare DB tab. Choose material, colour and location, and type what you paid.
  3. Weigh it. Put the spool on a kitchen scale and type the gross grams into Gross weight now (g). Grams left, percent left, cost per gram and value left are calculated, plus a LOW or CHECK TARE flag. Re-weigh whenever you put a spool away; overwrite the number.
  4. Dry Box Log tab. Log the date, box name, humidity percentage and whether you changed desiccant. Readings above your warning threshold are flagged HIGH.
  5. Summary tab. Total spools, grams and dollar value on hand grouped by material, count of low-stock spools, and dry-box warning count, with a bar chart of stock by material. Four sample spools are prefilled so you can see it working; overwrite or clear them.

Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
#NAME? or #SPILL! in result cellsYour Excel is older than 2016, or a function is not supported. Use the Google Sheets copy (link on page 1), update Excel, or open the file in LibreOffice Calc (free).
"The cell you're trying to change is protected"Formula cells are locked so they cannot be overwritten by accident. Only the yellow input cells are meant to be edited. If you really want to change a formula: Review → Unprotect Sheet (Excel) or Data → Protect sheets and ranges (Sheets). There is no password.
My spool brand is missing from the drop-downWeigh an empty spool of that brand once and add a row in the yellow cells at the bottom of the Tare DB tab (it joins the drop-down automatically), or pick Generic cardboard (fallback) / Generic plastic (fallback) and type your measured weight in Tare override (g). Submit the measurement at meshkit.pro/empty-spool-weight-database so it joins the next version.
Grams left shows 0The gross weight is at or below the tare, so either the scale was not zeroed, the wrong spool type is selected, or the spool really is empty. Re-weigh and check the brand row.
Percent left is above 100% or flag says CHECK TARECalculated grams left exceeds 105% of nominal net weight: either the manufacturer overfilled the spool or the tare is too low for this batch. Enter your own measured tare in Tare override (g).
I also want metres leftThe sheet tracks grams; convert grams to metres for any material and diameter with the free tool at meshkit.pro/filament-grams-to-meters.
